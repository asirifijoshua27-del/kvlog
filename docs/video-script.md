# KVLOG overview video: script

About 7 minutes. **[SCREEN]** = what to record. **VO** = what you say. Record at 1920×1080; zoom in on small controls.

---

## 1. Hook (0:00–0:30)

**[SCREEN]** A real service: the projector showing lyrics, the KVLOG Live Studio on the operator PC, the livestream on a phone.

**VO:** Lyrics, the Bible, the projector, the stage screen and your livestream: one app runs all of it. This is KVLOG, a free church presentation app made in Ghana for the way our churches run a service.

## 2. Why we made it (0:30–1:00)

**[SCREEN]** You at the media desk, then the KVLOG logo.

**VO:** I'm Joshua, media head at CACI Konongo. Every Sunday our team was jumping between three programs: one for lyrics, one for the Bible and OBS for the stream. Songs in Twi and English didn't fit, and when something went wrong there was nobody local to call. So we built KVLOG.

## 3. Running a service (1:00–2:30)

**[SCREEN]** Service Planner: drag songs, a Bible reading and announcements into order. Click **Open in Live Studio**.

**VO:** You plan the service once. Songs, scripture, announcements, videos and PowerPoint slides all go into one running order.

**[SCREEN]** Live Studio. Click a slide with *Click = Preview* on, then press **Take**.

**VO:** In Live Studio you see what's live, what's next and what OBS is sending. Queue a slide in Preview, check it, then press Enter to take it live. Nothing reaches the audience by accident.

**[SCREEN]** Press **B** (Blackout), then **Esc** (Clear). Show the stage display on a second screen.

**VO:** B is Blackout and Esc clears the screen. The stage display shows the band and the pastor the current words, the next slide, the time and the item's notes.

## 4. OBS in the same window (2:30–3:45)

**[SCREEN]** The OBS panel in Live Studio: click scene tiles, move an audio fader, mute the mic, open the large Program view.

**VO:** Your livestream is right here. Switch OBS scenes, watch the audio levels, mute a microphone and see exactly what's going out on the stream, without leaving KVLOG.

**[SCREEN]** Broadcast → Automatic scene switching: Song → "Lyrics", Slides → "Pastor camera". Send a song live; OBS switches by itself.

**VO:** You can also let KVLOG switch scenes for you. When a song goes live, OBS moves to the lyrics scene. When the sermon starts, it cuts to the pastor's camera. One less job for the volunteer.

**[SCREEN]** Display Manager → OBS look: a lower-third template for OBS while the room keeps full-screen lyrics.

**VO:** The stream can even have its own look: lyrics at the bottom over the camera online, full screen in the room.

## 5. Twi and English together (3:45–4:30)

**[SCREEN]** Presentation Studio: a song with English lyrics, then the *Second language* box with the Twi words. Switch between *Line by line* and *Side by side*; show the projector each time.

**VO:** Many of our songs are sung in two languages. In KVLOG you type the Twi words under the same verse and chorus labels, and each English line is shown with its Twi line. Pick line by line, or side by side.

## 6. For the band (4:30–5:15)

**[SCREEN]** Type `[G]Amazing [C]grace` in the lyrics. The projector shows only the words. Switch the stage display to the *Band* layout; the chords are above the words. Press *Band plays in* +2: the chords move up.

**VO:** Chords go straight into the lyrics. The congregation never sees them, but the band sees them on the stage display, with the key. If the singer needs it higher, move the key up and every chord changes.

**[SCREEN]** Give an item a planned length of 1 minute. Show the stage timer counting down, then turning red.

**VO:** Each item can have a planned time. The stage display counts down and turns red when it runs over, so the pastor and the band can see it.

## 7. Moving to KVLOG (5:15–6:00)

**[SCREEN]** Presentation Studio → Import songs → Import a whole folder. The list of found songs, with tags such as *In library* and *2 languages*. Click *Add*.

**VO:** You don't need to type your songs again. KVLOG imports from EasyWorship, ProPresenter, OpenLP, OpenLyrics, ChordPro, Word, PowerPoint and plain text. You check the list first, and songs you already have are skipped.

**[SCREEN]** Settings → Back up now to a USB stick. Then show *Daily backups*.

**VO:** Your work is safe. KVLOG backs itself up every day, and one click copies everything (songs, services, pictures and videos) to a USB stick, ready for a new computer.

## 8. Extras (6:00–6:45)

**[SCREEN]** Quick cuts, about 5 seconds each:
1. Toolbar → **Alert** → "Parent of child at the nursery…" appears on the projector and stage display.
2. Overlays: logo, lower third with the pastor's name, countdown before the service.
3. Slide Editor: a background photo with the *Warm* look and *Darken*.
4. Display Manager → **Show guide on screens**: raise the margins until a TV that cuts off the edges shows the whole picture.

**VO:** And the small things that matter on a Sunday: one-tap alerts for the nursery or the car park, your logo and lower thirds, a countdown before the service, picture filters for backgrounds, and screen margins for TVs that cut off the edges.

## 9. Close (6:45–7:15)

**[SCREEN]** The KVLOG logo, the download address and your WhatsApp number.

**VO:** KVLOG is free to download for Windows. If your church wants help setting it up, or there's something you need it to do, message us on WhatsApp. Download link below. God bless you.

## Recording checklist

- Use the sample service, or a test service with songs you are allowed to show. Don't show copyrighted lyrics on screen for long.
- Hide personal files and notifications (Windows: Focus assist / Do not disturb).
- Increase Windows display scaling to 125% for the recording, so text is readable on phones.
- Record the projector and stage display with a phone, or as a second OBS scene, so viewers see both screens.
- Captions: add English subtitles and a Twi version if you can. Many people watch with the sound off.
- Thumbnail: the KVLOG logo, a projector showing Twi and English lyrics, and the text "Free church app made in Ghana".
