# KVLOG: benefits and features

KVLOG is a free church presentation app for Windows, made in Ghana by the media team of CACI Konongo.

## Benefits

- **One app for the whole service:** lyrics, Bible, announcements, videos, PowerPoint, the projector, the stage screen and the livestream.
- **Made for Ghanaian churches:** Twi and English songs together, local Bibles you are allowed to use, and local support.
- **Fewer mistakes on a Sunday:** you check a slide in Preview before taking it live, and Blackout and Clear are one key each.
- **Volunteers can learn it quickly:** clear screens, plain language and keyboard shortcuts.
- **A better livestream:** OBS is controlled from inside KVLOG and can switch scenes by itself.
- **The band and pastor stay in step:** words, chords, the next slide, notes and the time on the stage display.
- **Easy to move in:** existing songs import from other programs.
- **Your work is safe:** saving is automatic, there are daily backups, and a full backup fits on a USB stick.
- **Works without internet:** everything is stored on the church PC.
- **Free to download, with automatic updates.**

## Features

### Service and presenting
- Service Planner: build, reorder, date and copy services.
- Live Studio: Live, Next/Preview and OBS monitors, with Click = Live or Click = Preview, and Take (Enter).
- Next and previous slide, Blackout (B), Clear (Esc), a full-screen projector (F5).
- Fade or cut transitions.
- The side menu can be hidden (Ctrl + \\).

### Songs
- Verse and chorus labels, repeating a chorus, and a choice of 1–4 lines per slide.
- Twi + English (or any two languages), line by line or side by side.
- Chords for the band, with the key and transposing.
- CCLI number, key and author for each song.
- **Song import:** text, OpenLyrics, OpenLP, EasyWorship, ProPresenter, ChordPro, Word and PowerPoint, one file or a whole folder at once.

### Bible
- Find a verse quickly, show it live, or add it to the service.
- Free downloads: KJV, WEB, ASV, BBE.
- Import other translations you are allowed to use (Zefania, OSIS, JSON).
- Two translations side by side.

### Media and presentations
- Pictures and videos as service items, or as moving backgrounds.
- PowerPoint and PDF import.
- A media library that flags missing files.

### Slide design
- Slide Editor: move text, add pictures and shapes, layers, lock and hide.
- 8 built-in templates plus your own, applied to all slides or to one slide.
- Picture filters: Warm, Cool, Faded and Dramatic looks, brightness, contrast, colour, blur, darken, black and white.

### Overlays and alerts
- Logo, lower third, clock, countdown, scrolling ticker, song/verse bar, picture-in-picture video.
- Choose for each overlay whether it shows on the projector, OBS, or both.
- One-tap alerts (nursery, car park) on the projector and stage display.

### OBS livestream
- Scene tiles, audio meters, faders and mute buttons.
- The OBS program picture, with a large view.
- Start and stop the stream and recording.
- Automatic scene switching for each item or item type.
- KVLOG output in OBS: full screen, or text over the camera, with its own template.

### Screens
- Choose a screen for the projector and the stage display, and identify the screens.
- Safe margins for each screen, with an on-screen guide.
- Stage display layouts: Standard, Band, Pastor or Words only, plus text size.
- Planned length for each item, and a stage timer that turns red when an item runs over.

### Reports, backup and updates
- Song usage report (CCLI or similar), saved as a CSV file for Excel.
- Full backup and restore to a folder or USB stick.
- Daily automatic backups, the last 14 days kept.
- Automatic updates that never restart KVLOG during a service.
- A Windows installer, with OBS passwords stored encrypted.
